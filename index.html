Selected Research

Bayesian Sampling for CUORE and CUPID

Adapted microcanonical Langevin Monte Carlo (MCLMC) to experimental likelihoods, with my own implementation and parameter transformation, and developed a block variant. Benchmarked both against Metropolis-Hastings and HMC in BAT.jl on CUORE data: posterior agreement, convergence, effective sample size per wall-clock second, and scaling with the number of datasets.

PAWS: Weakly Supervised Anomaly Detection

PAWS extends cross-section sensitivity by a factor of 10 over previous weakly supervised anomaly detection methods on the LHC Olympics benchmark, and remains robust to added irrelevant features. I implemented and benchmarked the weakly supervised classifiers, processing over 100 million simulated QCD events on NERSC GPU clusters. The research was published in Physical Review Letters, and I presented it in an invited talk at ML4Jets 2025 at Caltech.

Bayesian Supersymmetry

Ongoing work with Hitoshi Murayama on Bayesian parameter inference for supersymmetric models (CMSSM, GSUGRA and AMSB). Starting from high scale parameters, the renormalization group equations determine the sparticle spectrum, which is compared with experimental data to infer the supersymmetry breaking scale and the other model parameters.
