Research

Bayesian inference & sampling

Benchmarking Hamiltonian Monte Carlo and microcanonical Langevin Monte Carlo for high-dimensional experimental likelihoods, with emphasis on convergence, posterior agreement, effective sample size, and computational scaling.

Machine learning for particle physics

Worked on weakly supervised anomaly detection using large simulated collider datasets and NERSC GPU systems. This research resulted in a Physical Review Letters publication and an invited ML4Jets talk at Caltech.

Theory & phenomenology

Computational research in supersymmetry and chiral gauge theories, including Bayesian model studies and numerical methods contributing to two Physical Review D publications.
