Selected Research

Scalable Bayesian Inference for Experiments

Why is the universe made of matter instead of antimatter?

CUORE and CUPID search for neutrinoless double beta decay, a process that would show nature doesn't conserve lepton number, a key ingredient in leading explanations for why matter won out over antimatter. Finding that signal means sampling a posterior with over a hundred parameters across dozens of datasets, and standard samplers slow down sharply as data grows. My exact block gradient method exploits the likelihood's structure so the cost grows linearly rather than quadratically with the number of datasets.

Machine Learning for New Physics Searches

How do you search for new physics when you don't know what it looks like?

Weakly supervised anomaly detection can search for new physics without specifying exactly what it looks like, but it loses sensitivity when the signal is rare or the data has many irrelevant features. PAWS builds knowledge of a broad class of signal models into the search, matching the sensitivity of a fully supervised search without fixing the signal's parameters in advance. Benchmarked on over 100 million simulated events on NERSC GPUs, the work was published in Physical Review Letters, and I presented it in an invited talk at ML4Jets 2025. Our follow up paper, Generator Based Inference (under review), extends the approach to estimating physical parameters and their uncertainties.

Symmetry Breaking and the Origin of Mass

If supersymmetry exists, how and at what energy was it broken, and why do fermions have such different masses?

Quantum field theory lets physics at the highest energies be traced down to what we can measure today. Supersymmetry could explain why the Higgs boson is so light, but if it exists it must be broken, and how and at what energy that happens determines what experiments can see. Because the breaking must also respect the observed pattern of quark and lepton masses and mixings, it connects to the puzzle of why fermion masses differ so dramatically. Strongly coupled chiral gauge theories resist direct calculation, but starting from their supersymmetric versions and gently breaking supersymmetry with anomaly mediation makes their symmetry breaking patterns calculable, even for exceptional groups like E6. Ongoing work takes the data driven route: a Bayesian analysis that uses the renormalization group to evolve each model from the Planck scale down to the weak scale, across about seventeen orders of magnitude in energy, then confronts the predicted spectrum, Higgs mass, and flavor constraints with data to infer the supersymmetry breaking scale.

Real-Time Instrumentation

How precisely can we test the Standard Model?

MOLLER will measure the weak force between electrons precisely enough to probe physics beyond the Standard Model, which demands fast readout with very little noise. In Yury Kolomensky's group, we tested whether an AMD RFSoC 4x2 could serve as MOLLER's real-time data acquisition system, probing its signal chain, including digital down conversion, and its limits: noise against thermal and quantization floors, intermodulation distortion, and crosstalk between channels. The project was part of AMD's Open Hardware Adaptive Computing competition.
